1. **Gunning Fog Index (`gunning_fog`).** 
The Gunning Fog Index is included as a structural 
readability feature to capture variation in sentence 
length and the proportion of complex, polysyllabic 
words. These characteristics may differ systematically 
across authors and therefore provide potentially useful 
signals for writing-style change detection. Previous 
research on stylometric feature selection and 
writing-style-change detection has considered readability 
measures as features for characterising structural 
differences in text. However, this broader evidence 
does not establish that the Gunning Fog Index is among 
the most effective individual features for the PAN MAWSA 
task. Its contribution will therefore be assessed 
empirically within the proposed feature set.

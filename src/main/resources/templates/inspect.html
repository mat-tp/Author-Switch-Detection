Feature settings:

Sentences and Pairs (all sentences)

Per sentence: 63 scalar features, shown as extracted (raw) and as a z-score (value − training mean) / training std (cut at ±zClip), which is what the model actually sees. The 0 profile buckets (hashed character n-grams, hashed POS-tag n-grams, function words) are not listed one by one; they enter the pair vector through 4 measures per profile (cosine, Jensen–Shannon distance, total variation, support overlap).
Between sentences: the pair vector given to the model, 0 numbers: the scalar columns (by default abs_x = |z_x(A) − z_x(B)| for each of the scalar features), the measures of every profile, and overall_distance, the root mean square of the z-score differences. Values are shown as computed, not scaled.

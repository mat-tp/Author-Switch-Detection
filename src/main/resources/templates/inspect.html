Per sentence: 9 scalar features, shown as extracted (raw) and as a z-score (value − training mean) / training std, which is what the model actually sees. The 256 hashed character-trigram buckets are not listed one by one; they enter the pair vector through the ngram_cosine_similarity.
Between sentences: the pair vector given to the model: diff_x = |z_x(A) − z_x(B)| for each of the 9 features, ngram_cosine_similarity (1 = same trigram profile), and overall_distance = sqrt( Σ (z_x(A) − z_x(B))² / 9 ). Values are shown as computed, not scaled.
